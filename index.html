Weight estimate: APLS (UK) formulas, 0–12 months: (0.5 × age in months) + 4; 1–5 years: (2 × age) + 8; 6–12 years: (3 × age) + 7. EPALS/ERC formula (1–10 years): (age + 4) × 2; it tends to give lower weights in older children. Use the actual weight whenever you can, and ideal body weight for obese children where guidance says so.

Badges: MAX = capped at the maximum single dose. VERIFY = this dose couldn't be checked against an online national source when the app was built. Confirm it against BNFc or the local guideline before use.
